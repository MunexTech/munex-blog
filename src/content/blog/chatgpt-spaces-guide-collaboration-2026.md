OpenAI just shipped what might be the most significant ChatGPT update since GPT-4. It's called ChatGPT Spaces, and it fundamentally changes how teams can work with AI.

I got access during the DevDay rollout, and after a week of intensive testing with my team, I can confidently say: this is OpenAI's serious play for enterprise productivity software. Let me walk you through everything you need to know.

## What Is ChatGPT Spaces?

ChatGPT Spaces is a shared workspace where teams collaborate alongside ChatGPT and OpenAI's new Dots AI agents. Think of it as Google Docs meets Slack meets AI—a central hub where humans and AI work on projects together in real time.

Here's what makes it different from regular ChatGPT:

- **Shared context**: Everyone on the project sees the same conversation history and documents
- **Persistent workspace**: Your work lives in one place, organized by project
- **AI agents that work**: Dots can research, write, edit, and manage tasks without constant prompting
- **Native documents**: Pages and Slides are built-in, not bolted on

OpenAI clearly built this to compete with Anthropic's Claude Cowork, and honestly? They've delivered something impressive.

## Getting Started with ChatGPT Spaces

### Who Has Access?

As of November 2026, Spaces is available to:
- **Pro** subscribers ($200/month)
- **Business** teams ($25/user/month)
- **Enterprise** organizations (custom pricing)

Desktop and web access is live now; mobile is rolling out over the coming weeks.

### Creating Your First Space

Setting up a Space takes about 30 seconds:

1. Open ChatGPT on desktop or web
2. Click **"New Space"** in the sidebar
3. Give it a name and description
4. Invite team members via email
5. Start collaborating

Each Space acts as an isolated environment. You can have a Space for product development, another for marketing campaigns, and another for research—keeping contexts separate and organized.

## Pages: Where Documents Come Alive

Pages is OpenAI's native document format, and it's surprisingly capable. Unlike attaching Google Docs or Word files, Pages are born in ChatGPT and understand AI natively.

### What Can Pages Do?

- **Text and formatting**: Standard document features you'd expect
- **Embedded images**: Including AI-generated visuals from DALL-E
- **Interactive charts**: Data visualizations that update with your data
- **File attachments**: PDFs, spreadsheets, and other documents
- **Comments**: Team members and ChatGPT can leave inline comments
- **AI assistance**: ChatGPT or Dots can edit, expand, or rewrite any section

### Real-World Example: Creating a Market Analysis

Here's how I used Pages for a recent competitive analysis:

1. Started a conversation: "Create a market analysis Page for the project management software space"
2. ChatGPT generated a structured document with sections for market size, competitors, trends, and opportunities
3. I asked Dots to research specific competitors and add findings to relevant sections
4. Team members added comments and suggestions
5. Another Dots agent compiled everything into an executive summary

The entire process took about 2 hours. Doing this manually would have been a full day's work.

### Tips for Effective Pages

- **Be specific about structure**: Tell ChatGPT exactly what sections you want
- **Use version history**: Pages automatically saves versions—review them before sharing
- **Leverage comments**: Use @mentions to get AI input on specific sections
- **Export when needed**: Download as Word, PDF, or Markdown for external sharing

## Slides: Presentations Without the Pain

If Pages is the Google Docs competitor, Slides is going after PowerPoint. And honestly, it might be better for many use cases.

### How ChatGPT Slides Work

You create presentations through conversation:

> "Create a 10-slide presentation on our Q3 product roadmap. Include sections for completed features, upcoming releases, and next quarter's priorities."

ChatGPT generates a complete deck with:
- Professional layouts
- Relevant visuals and icons
- Consistent branding (if you've set brand guidelines)
- Speaker notes

### Advanced Slides Features

- **Conversational design**: "Make slide 3 more visual" or "Add a comparison table to slide 7"
- **Brand templates**: Upload your brand kit and all slides match automatically
- **Data integration**: Connect to your analytics and let Slides pull fresh numbers
- **Present mode**: Present directly from ChatGPT without exporting
- **Export options**: PowerPoint and Google Slides formats

### What I Loved About Slides

The iteration speed is incredible. Normally, building a polished 15-slide deck takes me 3-4 hours. With Slides, I had a solid first draft in 10 minutes, and after 30 minutes of refinement, it was client-ready.

The conversational interface means you're never hunting through menus. Need to change something? Just describe what you want.

## Dots: AI Agents That Actually Get Things Done

This is the feature that excites me most. Dots are AI agents that work within your Space, handling tasks autonomously while you focus on other work.

### What Can Dots Do?

Currently, Dots are capable of:

- **Research**: Browsing the web, reading documents, synthesizing information
- **Writing and editing**: Drafting content, reviewing documents, suggesting improvements  
- **Data analysis**: Processing spreadsheets, creating visualizations, identifying patterns
- **Task coordination**: Breaking down projects, tracking progress, managing timelines
- **Integration work**: Connecting with tools via MCP (Model Context Protocol)

### Example: Setting Up a Research Dot

Here's how I configured a Dot for competitive intelligence:

1. In my Space, created a new Dot called "CompetitorWatch"
2. Gave it instructions: "Monitor competitor websites weekly. Track pricing changes, new features, and major announcements. Summarize findings every Friday."
3. Connected relevant data sources
4. Let it run

Now I get weekly updates without lifting a finger. The Dot notices things I'd miss—like a subtle pricing page change or a new integration announcement buried in a changelog.

### Dots vs. GPTs

If you've used Custom GPTs, you might wonder how Dots differ:

| Feature | GPTs | Dots |
|---------|------|------|
| Where they run | Any ChatGPT conversation | Within Spaces only |
| Autonomy | Respond to prompts | Work independently on tasks |
| Memory | Conversation-limited | Persistent across sessions |
| Team access | Shareable but individual | Native team collaboration |
| Tool integration | Actions/plugins | Full MCP connector support |

Dots are essentially autonomous agents, while GPTs are specialized chat assistants. Both have their place, but for team workflows, Dots are the clear winner.

## Private Intelligence: Enterprise Security That Makes Sense

OpenAI knew that Spaces needed enterprise-grade security to compete for business users. Enter Private Intelligence—a system designed to give businesses confidence that their data stays private.

### What Private Intelligence Includes

- **Data isolation**: Your Space data is encrypted and separated from OpenAI's training
- **Audit logs**: Complete visibility into who accessed what and when
- **Retention controls**: Set data retention policies that match your compliance requirements
- **SSO integration**: Connect to your existing identity provider
- **Admin controls**: Granular permissions for Space creation, agent access, and exports

For enterprise customers, this addresses the biggest concern about using AI for sensitive work. Your competitive strategy or financial data stays within your organization's boundaries.

## Tips for Success with Spaces

After extensive use, here's what I've learned:

### Do This

- **Name Spaces clearly**: Future you will thank present you
- **Set up Dots early**: They take time to configure but save hours later
- **Use comments liberally**: They create a conversation trail that's valuable for decisions
- **Export important work**: Don't let critical documents live only in Spaces
- **Review Dots regularly**: Check their work periodically to ensure quality

### Avoid This

- **Don't create too many Spaces**: Start with a few focused ones
- **Don't skip the onboarding**: OpenAI's guides are actually helpful
- **Don't expect perfection**: AI-generated content still needs human review
- **Don't ignore permissions**: Set them up properly from the start

## ChatGPT Spaces vs. Claude Cowork

The inevitable comparison. Both OpenAI and Anthropic are racing to own the AI-powered workspace category. Here's how they stack up:

| Feature | ChatGPT Spaces | Claude Cowork |
|---------|---------------|---------------|
| Documents | Pages (native) | Claude Docs (native) |
| Presentations | Slides (native) | Claude Slides (native) |
| Agents | Dots | Cowork tasks |
| Team collaboration | Yes | Yes |
| Pricing | Pro/Business/Enterprise | Pro/Max/Ultra/Enterprise |
| Unique strength | MCP integrations | Unified chat + workspace |

Both are excellent. My take: Spaces has better integrations and more powerful agents, while Claude's unified experience is more elegant. Try both and see which fits your workflow better.

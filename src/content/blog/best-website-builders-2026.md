You don't need to code to build a professional website in 2026. But with dozens of website builders available, choosing the right one matters. Here's my breakdown.

## Top Website Builders Compared

### 1. Wix — Most Beginner-Friendly

Drag-and-drop that actually works.

**Strengths:**
- Intuitive editor
- 800+ templates
- App market
- AI website builder

**Best for:** Small businesses, portfolios, beginners

**Pricing:** Free (Wix ads), paid from $16/month

### 2. Squarespace — Best Design

Templates so beautiful you'll question your taste.

**Strengths:**
- Award-winning templates
- Built-in analytics
- E-commerce included
- Great for creatives

**Best for:** Photographers, artists, restaurants

**Pricing:** From $16/month

### 3. Shopify — Best for E-commerce

The gold standard for online stores.

**Strengths:**
- Powerful e-commerce
- Abandoned cart recovery
- Multi-channel selling
- App ecosystem

**Best for:** Online stores, dropshipping

**Pricing:** From $29/month

### 4. WordPress.com — Most Flexible

The CMS that powers 40% of the web.

**Strengths:**
- Unlimited customization
- Plugin ecosystem
- SEO-friendly
- Scales infinitely

**Best for:** Blogs, content sites, anyone who wants control

**Pricing:** Free tier, paid from $4/month

### 5. Webflow — Best for Designers

Visual development for pros.

**Strengths:**
- Design freedom
- CMS capabilities
- Clean code output
- Interactions/animations

**Best for:** Designers, agencies, complex sites

**Pricing:** Free tier, paid from $14/month

### 6. Carrd — Best for Simple Sites

One-page sites in minutes.

**Strengths:**
- Dead simple
- Perfect for landing pages
- Incredibly cheap
- Fast loading

**Best for:** Personal sites, landing pages, link-in-bio

**Pricing:** Free (3 sites), Pro from $19/year

### 7. Framer — Best for Startups

Modern, fast, interactive sites.

**Strengths:**
- Lightning fast
- Built-in CMS
- Component-based
- Great templates

**Best for:** Startups, SaaS, modern brands

**Pricing:** Free tier, paid from $5/month

## How to Choose

### What's your goal?

- **Sell products** → Shopify
- **Portfolio/creative** → Squarespace
- **Blog/content** → WordPress
- **Business site** → Wix or Squarespace
- **Landing page** → Carrd
- **Startup** → Framer or Webflow

### What's your skill level?

- **Complete beginner** → Wix or Carrd
- **Some design sense** → Squarespace
- **Technical/designer** → Webflow or WordPress

### What's your budget?

- **Free/cheap** → Carrd, WordPress.com free
- **Mid-range** → Wix, Squarespace
- **Investment** → Shopify, Webflow

## Common Mistakes

1. **Overbuilding** — Start simple, expand as needed
2. **Ignoring mobile** — Always preview on mobile
3. **Slow loading** — Optimize images, limit animations
4. **No SEO basics** — Set titles, descriptions, URLs properly

## FAQ

### Which website builder is best for SEO?

WordPress offers the most SEO control. Squarespace and Wix have improved significantly.

### Can I switch builders later?

Yes, but it's painful. Content migrates; design doesn't. Choose thoughtfully.

### Do I need hosting separately?

No. All builders listed include hosting. WordPress.org (self-hosted) is different from WordPress.com.

### Which is cheapest?

Carrd at $19/year. WordPress.com free works for basic blogs.

## Conclusion

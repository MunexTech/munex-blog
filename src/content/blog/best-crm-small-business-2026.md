Choosing the right CRM can make or break your small business growth. Here's my breakdown of the best CRM software for small businesses in 2026.

## Top 7 CRM Solutions

### 1. HubSpot CRM — Best Free Option

**Free features:** Contact management, deal tracking, email templates, meeting scheduling.

**Paid from:** $20/month per user

**Best for:** Startups, solopreneurs

### 2. Zoho CRM — Best Value

Enterprise features at small business prices.

**Pricing:** Free for 3 users, paid from $14/user/month

### 3. Salesforce Essentials — Best for Scaling

The industry standard, sized for small business.

**Pricing:** $25/user/month

### 4. Pipedrive — Best for Sales Teams

Visual, drag-and-drop pipeline management.

**Pricing:** From $14/user/month

### 5. Freshsales — Best All-in-One

Built-in phone, email, and CRM in one platform.

**Pricing:** Free tier, paid from $15/user/month

### 6. Monday Sales CRM — Most Flexible

Highly customizable workflows for unique needs.

**Pricing:** From $12/user/month

### 7. Less Annoying CRM — Simplest Option

No feature bloat. One simple price: $15/user/month.

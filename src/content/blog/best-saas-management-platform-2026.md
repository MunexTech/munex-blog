# Best SaaS Management Platforms 2026: Zluri vs Torii vs BetterCloud (IT Guide)

Last year, I discovered our 200-person company was paying for 47 unused Notion licenses. Nobody had left Notion—people just stopped using it while we kept paying. That single discovery paid for our SaaS management platform for two years.

This is the reality of SaaS sprawl in 2026. The average company uses 130+ SaaS applications, and IT can't manually track what's used, what's wasted, and what's a security risk. SaaS management platforms solve this, but choosing between them requires understanding what each does best.

I've implemented and managed all three leading platforms: Zluri, Torii, and BetterCloud. Here's what IT managers actually need to know.

## Zluri: The Cost Optimizer

Zluri positions itself as the complete SaaS management solution, and for cost-focused IT teams, it delivers. The platform excels at finding waste and optimizing spend.

### What Makes Zluri Stand Out

**Discovery is comprehensive.** Zluri finds SaaS through SSO integration, browser extension, expense reports, email scans, and API connections. In our deployment, it found 30% more applications than we knew existed.

**License optimization is actionable.** Not just "you have unused licenses"—Zluri shows exactly who isn't using what, how long since last activity, and the dollar impact of reclaiming each license.

**Vendor management built-in.** Track contracts, renewal dates, pricing negotiations, and vendor relationships in one place. We've saved significantly by catching auto-renewals before they hit.

**Spend analytics are clear.** See total SaaS spend, per-department breakdown, cost trends over time, and benchmark against similar companies. Executive reporting that non-technical leaders understand.

### Real ROI Example

When we deployed Zluri at a 200-person company:

| Finding | Monthly Waste | Annual Savings |
|---------|---------------|----------------|
| Unused Notion licenses | $940 | $11,280 |
| Duplicate tools (3 PDF editors) | $400 | $4,800 |
| Departed employees with active licenses | $1,200 | $14,400 |
| Underused Salesforce licenses | $600 | $7,200 |
| **Total first-year savings** | | **$37,680** |

Zluri's annual cost was about $12,000. ROI: 3x in year one.

### Where Zluri Falls Short

**Automation isn't as strong.** Workflows exist but aren't as flexible as Torii's. Complex automation requires workarounds.

**Integration depth varies.** Major apps integrate well. Niche tools may have limited data or require manual tracking.

**Enterprise pricing.** Smaller companies (under 100 employees) may find Zluri more than they need.

## Torii: The Automation Platform

Torii takes a developer-friendly approach to SaaS management. If your IT team thinks in workflows and APIs, Torii feels natural.

### What Makes Torii Powerful

**Workflow automation is excellent.** Build complex if-then automations without code. Employee onboarding, offboarding, access reviews, license reclamation—automate entire processes.

**API-first architecture.** Everything Torii does is accessible via API. Build custom integrations, connect to internal tools, extend functionality.

**Plugin marketplace.** Community and official integrations extend Torii's reach. Faster time-to-value for common tools.

**Implementation is fast.** We had Torii running in a week. The platform discovers SaaS quickly and insights appear within days.

### Automation Examples

Workflows we've built in Torii:

**Offboarding automation:**
1. HR marks employee as departing in HRIS
2. Torii detects status change via integration
3. Access is revoked across 40+ applications
4. Licenses are reclaimed and reallocated
5. Report generated for compliance

**License reclamation:**
1. Weekly scan for 60+ days inactivity
2. User notified with 14-day warning
3. If no activity, license downgraded/removed
4. IT notified of changes
5. Spend dashboard updated

These run automatically. We don't touch them.

### Where Torii Falls Short

**Cost optimization is secondary.** Torii finds waste but doesn't emphasize it like Zluri. Vendor management is basic.

**Learning curve for non-technical users.** The flexibility that developers love can overwhelm non-technical IT staff.

**Security features are lighter.** Not as deep on compliance and security monitoring as BetterCloud.

## BetterCloud: The Security Focus

BetterCloud started as a Google Workspace management tool and evolved into a broader SaaS security platform. For teams where security and compliance come first, it's the strongest choice.

### What Makes BetterCloud Security-Focused

**Deep Google Workspace / Microsoft 365 management.** BetterCloud does more with these platforms than competitors. File sharing policies, DLP, admin auditing—enterprise security controls.

**Data loss prevention built-in.** Scan for sensitive data in cloud files, enforce sharing policies, detect potential data exfiltration. Security features that require separate tools elsewhere.

**Compliance workflows.** SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR compliance assistance. Automated access reviews, audit reports, evidence collection.

**User provisioning is excellent.** Automated onboarding/offboarding with security-first defaults. New employees get right access, departed employees lose it instantly.

## Implementation Considerations

### Prerequisites for Success

**SSO is almost required.** Without single sign-on, these platforms miss shadow IT and usage data. Deploy SSO first if you haven't.

**Executive buy-in matters.** SaaS management surfaces uncomfortable truths (wasted spend, security gaps). Leadership must be ready to act.

**IT bandwidth for setup.** Plan for 20-40 hours of IT time for implementation, depending on complexity.

### Common Implementation Pitfalls

**1. Expecting immediate magic.** Discovery takes 2-4 weeks to be comprehensive. Wait for full visibility before making decisions.

**2. Not acting on findings.** Platforms surface waste, but you must reclaim licenses. Schedule time for optimization actions.

**3. Ignoring department pushback.** When you cut licenses, people complain. Have executive support and communication plans ready.

**4. Over-automating initially.** Start with visibility. Add automation once you understand your SaaS landscape.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do SaaS management platforms discover shadow IT?

Multiple methods: SSO login analysis, browser extension (optional), expense report scanning, email receipt analysis, network traffic analysis, and API connections to known applications. Comprehensive discovery requires multiple data sources.

### Will employees know their app usage is being monitored?

Depends on your deployment. Browser extensions are visible. Backend discovery is invisible to users. Most organizations disclose SaaS monitoring in IT policies. Individual activity (beyond usage/non-usage) isn't typically tracked.

### How long until we see ROI?

First optimization opportunities appear within 2-4 weeks. Meaningful savings require action—reclaiming licenses, consolidating tools, renegotiating contracts. Most teams see positive ROI within 3-6 months.

### Can these platforms automatically remove employee access during offboarding?

Yes, all three integrate with HRIS systems and automate deprovisioning. BetterCloud and Torii have stronger automation here. Manual offboarding is also possible for smaller organizations.

### Do we need a dedicated team to manage this?

Not dedicated, but someone must own it. For companies under 500 employees, 5-10 hours/week during initial optimization, then 2-5 hours/week ongoing. Automation reduces manual effort over time.

## My Recommendation

After implementing all three platforms:

**Choose Zluri if** cost optimization is your primary driver. The platform pays for itself quickly and keeps paying through ongoing spend management.

**Choose Torii if** your IT team is technical and wants automation flexibility. The API-first approach enables customization that others can't match.

**Choose BetterCloud if** security and compliance are non-negotiable. The DLP and Google/Microsoft depth justify the premium for regulated industries.

For most growing companies (100-500 employees), I'd start with **Zluri or Torii** for cost optimization and automation, adding security tooling separately if needed. For enterprise security-conscious organizations, **BetterCloud** makes sense from day one.

The platform matters less than taking action. SaaS sprawl costs companies thousands monthly. Any of these platforms surfaces the waste—you just have to do something about it.

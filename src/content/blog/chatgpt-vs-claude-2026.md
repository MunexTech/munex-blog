The AI assistant war is heating up. OpenAI's ChatGPT and Anthropic's Claude are the two heavyweights. I've used both extensively—here's my honest comparison.

## The Quick Verdict

**ChatGPT** wins for versatility, plugins, and multimodal capabilities.
**Claude** wins for long-form content, nuanced responses, and complex instructions.

## Feature-by-Feature Comparison

### Context Window

| Model | Context Window |
|-------|----------------|
| ChatGPT (GPT-4o) | 128K tokens |
| Claude 3.5 Sonnet | 200K tokens |

**Winner: Claude** — Better for long documents.

### Multimodal Capabilities

| Feature | ChatGPT | Claude |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Image understanding | ✅ | ✅ |
| Image generation | ✅ DALL-E | ❌ |
| Voice | ✅ | ❌ Limited |
| Web browsing | ✅ | ❌ |

**Winner: ChatGPT**

### Pricing

Both offer free tiers and $20/month pro plans. Nearly identical.

## Real-World Use Cases

### For Writing Blog Posts

**Claude** for research and first drafts. **ChatGPT** for quick edits and variations.

### For Coding

Both excellent. ChatGPT has more integrations; Claude handles large codebases better.

## Which Should You Choose?

### Choose ChatGPT if:
- You want plugins and integrations
- Image generation matters
- You need versatility

### Choose Claude if:
- You work with long documents
- Writing quality is top priority
- You need nuanced responses

## FAQ

### Is Claude better than ChatGPT?

Neither is objectively better. Choose based on your use case.

### Can I use both?

Yes! Many professionals use both—$40/month for both subscriptions pays for itself.

## Conclusion

Use both free tiers to test. Pay for whichever you reach for more often.

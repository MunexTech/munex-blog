# Best HR Software for Small Business 2026: Gusto vs Rippling vs BambooHR

The first time I ran payroll manually, I spent four hours on calculations, made two errors, and still got a compliance notice from the state. That was the last time I tried to handle HR without proper software.

Small business HR has transformed. What used to require a dedicated HR person—or expensive outsourcing—now runs through software that handles payroll, benefits, onboarding, and compliance automatically. After implementing Gusto, Rippling, and BambooHR across different companies over the past five years, I have clear opinions about which works for whom.

Here's what actually matters when choosing HR software for your small business.

## Quick Verdict

**Gusto** — Best for small teams and simplicity. Excellent payroll, easy benefits, minimal learning curve.

**Rippling** — Best for fast-scaling startups. IT + HR unified, powerful automation, grows with you.

**BambooHR** — Best for established SMBs. HR-focused features, excellent employee experience, strong culture tools.

## Gusto: The Small Business Favorite

Gusto built its reputation on making payroll painless. For businesses under 50 employees who want comprehensive HR without complexity, it's the obvious choice.

### What Makes Gusto Perfect for Small Teams

**Payroll is genuinely easy.** Run payroll in minutes, not hours. Automatic tax calculations, filings, and payments. Year-end W-2s and 1099s generated automatically. I've run Gusto payroll from my phone while traveling—it just works.

**Benefits administration simplified.** Health insurance, 401(k), FSA, HSA, commuter benefits—Gusto brokers and administers everything. You don't need a benefits consultant.

**Onboarding that employees love.** New hires complete paperwork online before day one. I-9, W-4, direct deposit, benefits enrollment—handled. They show up ready to work instead of drowning in forms.

**Compliance peace of mind.** State registrations, tax filings, labor law posters, workers' comp—Gusto handles the compliance maze that trips up small businesses.

**Transparent, simple pricing.** $40/month base plus $6 per person. No hidden fees, no surprise charges. Know exactly what you'll pay.

### Real Gusto Experience

I've used Gusto for a 12-person services company:

| Metric | Before Gusto | With Gusto |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| Payroll processing time | 4+ hours/month | 20 minutes/month |
| Compliance issues/year | 2-3 | 0 |
| Benefits setup time | 3 weeks | 1 week |
| Employee onboarding | 2-3 days | Same-day |
| Monthly cost | ~$600 (outsourced) | ~$112 |

The ROI was immediate and obvious.

### Where Gusto Falls Short

**Limited beyond HR basics.** No IT management, basic performance reviews, minimal applicant tracking. As you grow, you'll add other tools.

**International is limited.** Contractor payments in some countries, but no international employee payroll. Global teams need Rippling or Deel.

**Performance management is basic.** Reviews exist but aren't sophisticated. Growing companies wanting robust performance tools look elsewhere.

**No unified IT.** Device management, app provisioning, security—handled separately. Rippling's unified approach is compelling for tech-forward teams.

## Rippling: The All-in-One Platform

Rippling is ambitious: unify HR, IT, and Finance into one system. For fast-scaling startups, this unified approach eliminates the tool sprawl that creates headaches.

### What Makes Rippling Powerful

**HR + IT unified.** When you hire someone in Rippling, they're automatically added to payroll, enrolled in benefits, provisioned a laptop, given app access, and set up in every system. When they leave, everything revokes automatically. This is transformative for growing teams.

**Automation is deep.** Build workflows: "When employee changes from IC to manager, enroll in management training, update permissions, notify finance." Complex processes run automatically.

**International payroll built-in.** Pay employees and contractors in 100+ countries through Rippling's entities. No third-party EOR needed for many countries.

**Scales without switching.** A 5-person startup and a 2,000-person company can use the same Rippling instance. You won't outgrow it.

**Integrations are extensive.** 500+ apps connect natively. Whatever your stack, Rippling probably syncs with it.

### The Rippling Experience

A client scaled from 15 to 80 employees over 18 months on Rippling:

What worked brilliantly:
- New hire onboarding dropped from 2 days to 2 hours
- Device provisioning became automatic
- App access tied to role, not manual requests
- International contractors added without new platforms
- One dashboard replaced four separate tools

What required adjustment:
- Initial setup was more complex than Gusto
- Pricing became significant at scale
- Some features were overkill for early stages

### Where Rippling Falls Short

**Pricing complexity.** Base rate is low, but modules add up. Payroll, benefits, IT, learning—each is additional. Get a comprehensive quote before committing.

**Overkill for small teams.** A 5-person company may not need unified IT + HR. The power justifies itself only as you scale.

**Learning curve is real.** More features mean more to learn. Plan for implementation time that Gusto doesn't require.

**Support is mixed.** Some report excellent support; others describe slow response times. Experience varies.

## BambooHR: The HR-Focused Platform

BambooHR focuses purely on HR—and does it exceptionally well. For established SMBs wanting great employee experience and HR depth, it's the specialist choice.

### What Makes BambooHR Excel

**Employee experience is central.** The employee self-service portal is best-in-class. PTO requests, benefits viewing, org charts, company announcements—employees love using it.

**Performance management is robust.** Goals, reviews, feedback, 1:1s—BambooHR provides the tools growing companies need for people development.

**Onboarding creates culture.** Pre-boarding, welcome messages, automated task lists, signature collection—new hires feel valued before they start.

**Applicant tracking built-in.** Post jobs, track candidates, schedule interviews, collaborate on hiring—without a separate ATS.

**Reporting is excellent.** Turnover analysis, headcount trends, compensation reports—data for strategic HR decisions.

### The BambooHR Difference

BambooHR shines for companies where HR is a strategic function, not just administrative:

- Employee engagement surveys with analytics
- Culture features (employee recognition, announcements)
- Performance review cycles that scale
- Compensation benchmarking data
- Strategic workforce planning

If "people operations" resonates more than "payroll processing," BambooHR speaks your language.

### Where BambooHR Falls Short

**Payroll requires integration.** BambooHR partners with providers but doesn't own payroll. You're adding another system.

**No IT management.** Device and app management are separate tools entirely.

**International is limited.** US-focused. International teams need additional solutions.

**Pricing is opaque.** Custom quotes only. Harder to budget before talking to sales.

**Less automation than Rippling.** Workflow automation exists but isn't as powerful.

### BambooHR Pricing

BambooHR doesn't publish pricing. Expect:

## Best HR Software by Company Size

### Solopreneurs and Tiny Teams (1-5): Gusto Simple

**Why:** $40 base + $6/person is affordable. Handles payroll, benefits, compliance without complexity. Don't overthink it.

**Setup tip:** Enable AutoPilot for fully hands-off payroll.

### Small Business (5-25): Gusto Plus

**Why:** Time tracking, PTO management, and team features become important. Gusto Plus adds these without overwhelming complexity.

**Setup tip:** Use the employee self-service features to reduce HR admin time.

### Scaling Startups (25-100): Rippling

**Why:** Growth creates complexity. Rippling's unified HR + IT prevents tool sprawl. Automation saves increasingly valuable time.

**Setup tip:** Implement during a calm period, not during rapid hiring. Setup takes investment.

### Established SMBs (50-500): BambooHR or Rippling

**Why:** BambooHR if HR/people development is the priority. Rippling if IT + HR unification matters more.

**Setup tip:** Involve HR leadership in platform selection. They'll live in it daily.

### International Teams: Rippling or Deel

**Why:** International payroll and compliance are complex. Rippling handles 100+ countries natively. Deel specializes in global employment.

**Setup tip:** Understand entity vs. EOR trade-offs before committing to a platform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I switch HR software mid-year?

Yes, but it's easier at year-end. Mid-year switches require careful tax document transfer to ensure accurate W-2s. Most platforms have migration support, but expect some manual work.

### What's the difference between Gusto and Rippling?

Gusto focuses on being the best simple HR platform. Rippling tries to unify HR, IT, and Finance. Small teams wanting ease choose Gusto. Scaling teams wanting integration choose Rippling.

### Is BambooHR worth it without built-in payroll?

Yes, if HR features beyond payroll matter to you. Performance management, employee engagement, and culture tools justify BambooHR even with separate payroll. But if payroll is your main need, Gusto is simpler.

### How much should small business HR software cost?

Budget $6-15 per employee per month for basic HR and payroll. Comprehensive solutions (HR + IT + benefits) run $25-40 per employee. Below 10 employees, flat-rate pricing like Gusto's is often cheaper.

### Do I need HR software if I only have contractors?

Full HR platforms are overkill. Use contractor payment tools (Deel, Gusto Contractor-Only, PayPal Business) instead. HR software makes sense when you have actual employees with payroll, benefits, and compliance needs.

## The Bottom Line

After implementing all three platforms across different companies:

**Choose Gusto** if you're a small team wanting payroll and HR that just works. The simplicity is the feature. You'll be running payroll confidently within days.

**Choose Rippling** if you're scaling fast and want to prevent tool sprawl. The HR + IT unification pays dividends as complexity grows. Plan for implementation investment.

**Choose BambooHR** if people development and employee experience are strategic priorities. It's the most HR-focused platform and shows in the depth of people features.

The right choice depends on your company stage and priorities. A 10-person team doesn't need Rippling's power. A 100-person company will outgrow Gusto's simplicity. Match the tool to where you are—and where you're going.

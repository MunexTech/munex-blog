Video meetings aren't going away. But Zoom fatigue is real, and alternatives have caught up. Here's my comparison of the best video conferencing software in 2026.

## Top Video Conferencing Platforms

### 1. Zoom — Still the Standard

Love it or hate it, Zoom remains the default for a reason.

**Strengths:**
- Rock-solid reliability
- Best-in-class features
- Universal familiarity
- Webinar capabilities

**Weaknesses:**
- Security concerns (mostly addressed)
- Subscription fatigue

**Pricing:** Free (40 min limit), Pro from $15.99/user/month

### 2. Google Meet — Best for Google Users

If you're in Google Workspace, Meet just works.

**Strengths:**
- Seamless Calendar integration
- No downloads needed (browser-based)
- Live captions
- Clean interface

**Weaknesses:**
- Fewer advanced features
- Requires Google account for hosts

**Pricing:** Free (60 min), included in Workspace ($6+/user/month)

### 3. Microsoft Teams — Best for Enterprise

The hub for Microsoft 365 users.

**Strengths:**
- Deep Office integration
- Channels and chat
- Enterprise features
- Included with M365

**Weaknesses:**
- Resource-heavy
- Complex for simple calls

**Pricing:** Free tier, included with M365 ($6+/user/month)

### 4. Webex — Best for Security

Cisco's enterprise-grade solution.

**Strengths:**
- Enterprise security
- AI features
- Hardware ecosystem
- Reliable performance

**Pricing:** Free tier, paid from $14.50/user/month

### 5. Around — Best for Async Teams

Designed for remote-first companies.

**Strengths:**
- Floating video bubbles
- Low bandwidth usage
- Async-friendly
- Minimal interface

**Pricing:** Free

### 6. Discord — Best for Communities

Not just for gaming anymore.

**Strengths:**
- Great for communities
- Persistent servers
- Screen sharing
- Completely free

**Pricing:** Free, Nitro from $9.99/month

## FAQ

### What's the best free video conferencing tool?

Google Meet for business (60 min free). Discord for unlimited casual calls.

### Which video tool uses least bandwidth?

Around is designed for low bandwidth. Zoom also performs well on poor connections.

### Can I use multiple video platforms?

Yes, most professionals use 2-3 depending on context and client preferences.

## Conclusion

- **Default choice:** Zoom (it just works)
- **Google Workspace users:** Google Meet
- **Microsoft 365 users:** Teams
- **Security-focused:** Webex
- **Remote-first:** Around
- **Communities:** Discord

Most are free for basic use. Pick what integrates best with your existing tools.

The AI assistant wars have officially entered a new phase. We're no longer comparing chatbots—we're comparing **AI agents** that work in the background, manage your apps, and actually do things while you sleep.

In the past month, three major players have launched their AI agent products: Anthropic's Claude Duet (October 21), OpenAI's ChatGPT Dots (September 29), and Meta's Muse (September 8). I've been testing all three, and the differences are significant.

Here's my honest take on which one deserves your subscription dollars.

## What Are AI Agents, Exactly?

Before we dive in, let's clarify what makes these different from regular ChatGPT or Claude.

Traditional AI chatbots **wait for your input**. You ask a question, they answer. You close the app, they stop working.

AI agents are **proactive**. They:
- Connect to your email, calendar, and business apps
- Work in the background on their own cloud computer
- Check for updates and handle tasks without being asked
- Send you a morning briefing of what happened overnight
- Ask for approval before taking sensitive actions

Think of them as a digital assistant that never clocks out.

## Claude Duet: The Professional's Choice

**Price:** Included with Claude Pro ($20/mo) or Max (from $100/mo)  
**Availability:** US only, waitlist

### What I Like

Anthropic positioned Duet as a "chief of staff" for professionals, and it shows. The **morning briefing** feature is genuinely useful—every morning, I get a digest of important emails, upcoming deadlines, and anything that needs my attention across my connected apps.

The **6,000+ app connections** are impressive. QuickBooks, Stripe, HubSpot, Gusto, DocuSign—basically anything a small business runs on. I connected my Stripe account and now Duet flags unusual transactions and summarizes my weekly revenue without me asking.

What really sets Duet apart is its **safety approach**. Before paying, sending, or deleting anything, it asks for explicit approval. Trading and moving money are completely off-limits. For business owners handling sensitive data, this matters.

### What I Don't Like

The **waitlist is frustrating**. Even with a Pro subscription, you're not guaranteed access. It's US-only, which cuts out a huge chunk of potential users.

It also only **checks your apps every few hours**, not continuously. If you need real-time monitoring, this might feel slow.

### My Verdict on Claude Duet

Best for solo founders and small business owners who already use Claude and need a trustworthy assistant for financial and administrative work. The $20 price point is unbeatable.

## ChatGPT Dots: The Power User's Dream

**Price:** Starts at $100/mo (ChatGPT Pro) or $125/mo (Business Premium)  
**Availability:** US, Canada (not available in EEA, UK, or Switzerland on Pro)

### What I Like

Dots is the most **capable** of the three. Each dot runs on GPT-6 Astra and gets its own cloud computer that you can actually watch working. You can have multiple dots running different projects simultaneously.

The **cross-platform presence** is killer. I can message my dot in ChatGPT, switch to Slack, and it has full context of what we were discussing. For teams, this is huge—you can bring a dot into a Slack channel and the whole team can interact with it.

OpenAI's **continuous proactive research** means your dot is always looking for relevant information in the background. Working on a competitor analysis? Your dot will keep surfacing new findings even when you're not actively prompting it.

### What I Don't Like

**$100 per month minimum** is steep. You're paying 5x what Claude Duet costs, and for many people, the extra features won't justify that.

The **geographic restrictions** are annoying. If you're in Europe, you need a Business Premium seat, which requires a 2-seat minimum at $125 each. That's $250/month just to get started.

Also, dots conversations are unlimited but **your regular ChatGPT usage still has limits** on the same plan.

### My Verdict on ChatGPT Dots

Best for teams already embedded in the ChatGPT ecosystem who need maximum capability and don't mind paying premium prices. The Slack/Teams integration alone might justify it for some organizations.

## Meta Muse: The People's Agent

**Price:** Free (basic), Power $20/mo, Maximum $100/mo  
**Availability:** US & Canada, 18+

### What I Like

Meta surprised everyone by making Muse **genuinely free** for basic use. You can connect it to Instagram, Facebook Pages, WhatsApp, and basic business tools without paying a cent.

For **creators and small retailers**, the Meta ecosystem integration is unmatched. Muse can manage your social media, respond to DMs, handle Shopify orders, and run Meta ads—all connected natively.

The **Sentinel agent** is a nice touch: a separate AI that gates Muse's internet access and prevents it from doing anything sketchy. It's like having a security guard watching your AI.

### What I Don't Like

The **"free" tier has limitations** that push you toward paid plans quickly. The moment you need real business automation, you're looking at $20/mo minimum.

Muse feels **consumer-focused**. If you're not running an Instagram business or selling on Shopify, a lot of its integrations aren't relevant. The 6,000 connectors Claude has vs. Muse's "dozens" tells the story.

I also have **privacy concerns** with Meta handling my business data. Your mileage may vary.

### My Verdict on Meta Muse

Best for creators, influencers, and small Shopify sellers who live in the Meta ecosystem. The free tier is worth trying, but serious business users will outgrow it fast.

## Which One Should You Choose?

After testing all three, here's my recommendation framework:

### Choose Claude Duet if:
- You run a small business with QuickBooks, Stripe, or similar tools
- Safety and approval workflows matter to you
- You want the lowest price for a capable AI agent
- You're in the US and can get off the waitlist

### Choose ChatGPT Dots if:
- Your team already uses ChatGPT Pro or Business
- You need multi-platform presence (Slack, Teams, ChatGPT)
- You want continuous background research, not just periodic check-ins
- Budget isn't your primary concern

### Choose Meta Muse if:
- Your business runs on Instagram, Facebook, or Shopify
- You want to try AI agents for free first
- You're a creator or influencer, not a traditional business
- You're comfortable with Meta's data practices

## My Personal Stack

For what it's worth, I'm currently using **Claude Duet** for business administration and **ChatGPT Dots** for research projects. The $120/month combined cost is significant, but having both gives me the best of both worlds: Duet's careful handling of financial tasks and Dots' relentless research capability.

If I had to pick just one? **Claude Duet at $20/month** offers the best value. It does 80% of what I need for 20% of the price.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use Claude Duet outside the US?
Not yet. Anthropic has only launched the research preview in the US for Pro and Max subscribers. No timeline for international expansion has been announced.

### Do ChatGPT Dots count against my usage limits?
Conversations with your dots don't count against your message limits. However, your regular ChatGPT usage on the same plan still has the standard caps.

### Is Meta Muse really free?
Yes, for basic use. You can connect social media accounts and get basic automation. But for serious business features like advanced scheduling and multi-account management, you'll need the Power ($20/mo) or Maximum ($100/mo) tier.

### Can these AI agents access my bank account?
Claude Duet explicitly blocks trading and moving money. ChatGPT Dots requires approval for financial actions. Meta Muse uses Sentinel to gate sensitive operations. All three have safety rails, but Claude's are the strictest.

### Which has the best AI model?
Claude Duet runs on Opus 5.5, which scores highest on the Artificial Analysis index (58). ChatGPT Dots uses GPT-6 Astra, which ties with Opus on automation benchmarks but has broader general knowledge. For most users, the difference is negligible.

## The Bottom Line

AI agents are the biggest shift in productivity tools since smartphones. All three of these products work well, but they're built for different users.

If you're unsure, start with **Meta Muse's free tier** to understand what AI agents can do. Then graduate to **Claude Duet** when you're ready for serious business automation. Only move to **ChatGPT Dots** if you need team collaboration or have specific power-user requirements that justify the price.

The AI agent era is here. The only question is which one will run your digital life.

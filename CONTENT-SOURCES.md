## Content Categories to Monitor

1. **AI Tool Launches** — New features, product updates
2. **Best Practices** — How-to guides, tutorials
3. **Comparisons** — X vs Y articles
4. **Industry Trends** — Market analysis, predictions
5. **Case Studies** — Success stories, use cases
6. **Pricing Changes** — Plan updates, new tiers

## Usage Notes

- Check 5-10 sources daily for fresh content ideas
- Look for trending topics across multiple blogs
- Focus on evergreen "how to" and "best of" content
- Prioritize high-CPC niches (AI, CRM, email marketing)
- Always add unique insights and personal experience
